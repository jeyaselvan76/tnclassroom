## **2\. Type I: Continuous or Repeating Patterns (தொடர் வரிசை அமைப்புகள்)**

In this type, a series of letters is given with blanks. These letters form a repeating group or a specific continuous pattern.

### **Steps to Solve:**

1. **Count the Total Spaces:** Count the letters and the blanks combined.  
2. **Divide into Groups:** Divide the total count into equal parts (e.g., if there are 12 spaces, divide into groups of 3 or 4).  
3. **Identify the Pattern:** Look for repeating sets of letters across the groups.

### **Examples:**

* **Example 1:** abc \_\_ abc \_\_ bbca \_\_ c \_\_ aaabc  
  * **Logic:** By observing the options and the distribution, the pattern follows a specific repetition of 'a', 'b', and 'c'.  
  * **Answer:** aabc  
* **Example 2:** bca \_ b \_ aabc \_ a \_ caa  
  * **Logic:** Observing the letters around the blanks to find a recurring sequence like "bcaa".  
  * **Answer:** acab

## **3\. Type II: Alphabetical Skipping and Positional Logic (எழுத்துக்களின் இடமதிப்பு மற்றும் இடைவெளி)**

These series rely on the mathematical difference between letter positions, alternating series, or combinations with numbers.

### **Common Patterns:**

1. **Fixed Skipping:** Adding or subtracting a constant number (e.g., \+2, \+2, \+2).  
2. **Increasing/Decreasing Skips:** Adding increasing numbers (e.g., \+1, \+2, \+3).  
3. **Fibonacci Logic:** The next letter's position is the sum of the previous two positions.  
4. **Alternating Series:** Two different patterns running in parallel at odd and even positions.

## 

## **4\. Solved Examples with Detailed Explanations**

### **Positional Logic (இடமதிப்புத் தொடர்பு)**

**Example 1: A, B, C, E, H, M, \_\_\_**

* **Explanation:**  
  * A \= 1, B \= 2, C \= 3, E \= 5, H \= 8, M \= 13\.  
  * Look at the numbers: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13... (Sum of previous two: 8+13 \= 21).  
  * The 21st letter is **U**.  
* **Tamil (விளக்கம்):** இது பிபோனச்சி வரிசையைப் போன்றது. முந்தைய இரண்டு எழுத்துக்களின் இடமதிப்பை கூட்டினால் அடுத்த எழுத்து வரும். 8 \+ 13 \= 21\. 21-வது எழுத்து **U**.

**Example 2: A, E, I, M, Q, \_\_\_**

* **Explanation:**  
  * A(1) \+ 4 \= E(5)  
  * E(5) \+ 4 \= I(9)  
  * I(9) \+ 4 \= M(13)  
  * M(13) \+ 4 \= Q(17)  
  * Q(17) \+ 4 \= U(21).  
* **Answer:** **U**

### **Multi-Letter Groups (கூட்டு எழுத்துத் தொடர்)**

**Example 3: BAT, DCV, FEX, \_\_\_**

* **Explanation:** Break the groups into first, second, and third letters.  
  * 1st Letters: B, D, F (+2 skipping) → **H**  
  * 2nd Letters: A, C, E (+2 skipping) → **G**  
  * 3rd Letters: T, V, X (+2 skipping) → **Z**  
* **Answer:** **HGZ**

**Example 4: B 12 C, E 15 E, H 18 G, \_\_\_, N 24 K**

* **Explanation:**  
  * First Letters: B(2) \+ 3 \= E(5); E(5) \+ 3 \= H(8); H(8) \+ 3 \= **K(11)**.  
  * Numbers: 12 \+ 3 \= 15; 15 \+ 3 \= 18; 18 \+ 3 \= **21**.  
  * Last Letters: C(3) \+ 2 \= E(5); E(5) \+ 2 \= G(7); G(7) \+ 2 \= **I(9)**.  
* **Answer:** **K 21 I**

### **Alternating Series (மாற்றுத் தொடர் வரிசை)**

**Example 5: Z, L, X, J, V, H, T, F, \_\_\_**

* **Explanation:** There are two series here.  
  * Series 1 (Positions 1, 3, 5, 7): Z, X, V, T (Backwards \-2: 26, 24, 22, 20). Next is 18\.  
  * Series 2 (Positions 2, 4, 6, 8): L, J, H, F (Backwards \-2: 12, 10, 8, 6).  
  * The missing term belongs to Series 1: 20 \- 2 \= 18\. The 18th letter is **R**.  
* **Answer:** **R**

## **5\. Tips and Shortcuts (குறிப்புகள் மற்றும் எளிதான வழிமுறைகள்)**

* **Write the Alphabet:** As soon as the exam starts, write A to Z with their numbers (1-26) and Z to A (26-1) on your rough work sheet.  
* **Alpha-Numeric Questions:** Solve letters and numbers separately. In questions like C4X, F9U, I16R, solve the numbers first (2², 3², 4² → 5² \= 25\) to narrow down options.  
* **Check First and Last:** Often, checking just the first letter of the answer groups helps you find the correct option quickly without solving the entire group.  
* **Product Logic:** Some complex series use the product of positions. For example, AB 2 (1×2=2) and EF 30 (5×6=30).
