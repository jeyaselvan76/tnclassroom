About Examination Tracking

Understanding Your Practice Test Performance

Continuous evaluation is vital for candidates appearing in competitive state exams such as the Tamil Nadu Teacher Eligibility Test (TNTET) and the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS) examination. Our real-time scoring engine logs unit-wise multiple-choice question (MCQ) attempts directly to a centralized cloud database, allowing students and educators to review learning trajectories over time.

Score Analysis

Scores with 50% or higher accuracy are highlighted in green, indicating solid conceptual retention. Scores under 50% indicate areas requiring textbook revision.
