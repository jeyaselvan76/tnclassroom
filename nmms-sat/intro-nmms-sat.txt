Welcome to the NMMS SAT Learning Hub — Complete Scholastic Aptitude Test Preparation
The National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS) Examination is a prestigious state-level competitive exam designed to identify and support meritorious Class 8 students across Tamil Nadu. Success in the NMMS exam requires strong performance in both the Mental Ability Test (MAT) and the Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT).

To help students excel, TN Classroom provides a dedicated, free, and structured online learning portal tailored specifically to the NMMS SAT curriculum.

Comprehensive Subject Coverage (Class 7 & Class 8 Syllabus)
The NMMS SAT section evaluates a student's core academic understanding of school subjects covered in Class 7 and Class 8. Our portal breaks down the entire official syllabus into manageable, high-yield learning units across three key subjects:

Mathematics (கணிதம்): Master foundational arithmetic, algebra, geometry, number systems, and measurement concepts through clear problem-solving steps and formulas.

Science (அறிவியல்): Explore Physics, Chemistry, and Biology concepts, complete with diagrams, formulas, definitions, and key scientific facts.

Social Science (சமூக அறிவியல்): Gain in-depth knowledge of History (ancient, medieval, and modern Indian history), Geography, Civics, and Economics.
