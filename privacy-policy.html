Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

At TN Classroom, accessible from www.tnclassroom.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by TN Classroom and how we utilize it.

Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us via our contact page.

Log Files

TN Classroom follows standard procedure by maintaining server log files. These files log visitors when they access the platform—a routine practice conducted by hosting service analytics. Information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click frequency. This information is not linked to any personally identifiable data and is used solely for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking navigation paths, and gathering broad demographic insights.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other educational portal, TN Classroom uses 'cookies'. These cookies store visitor preferences and track visited pages to optimize user experience by customizing web page content based on browser type and settings.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve targeted advertisements to our site visitors based on their visit to www.tnclassroom.com and other websites across the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Advertising Partners & Third-Party Privacy Policies

Some advertisers on our platform may use cookies and web beacons. Our primary advertising partner is Google AdSense. Third-party ad servers or networks use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links appearing on TN Classroom, sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this happens. These technologies measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and personalize the advertising content displayed.

Note: TN Classroom has no access to or control over cookies used by third-party advertisers. We recommend consulting the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for detailed operational information and instructions on opting out of specific practices.

Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We ensure that users are fully aware of all data protection rights. Every user is entitled to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of their personal feedback records. If you submit a request, we will respond within one month.

Children's Information (COPPA)

Protecting children while using the internet is an essential priority. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and monitor their online activity. TN Classroom does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such records from our systems.
