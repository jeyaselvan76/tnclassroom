Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about TNTET & NMMS study modules, PRO access, and score tracking on tnclassroom.com.

What is TN Classroom (tnclassroom.com)?

TN Classroom (www.tnclassroom.com) is an online learning portal providing Tamil Nadu State Board (Samacheer Kalvi) aligned study notes, video classes, and interactive online MCQ practice tests for TNTET (Paper 1 & Paper 2) and NMMS scholarship aspirants.

Are free practice tests and study notes available on TN Classroom?

Yes. Free introductory sessions (Session 1) are open without payment across all TNTET subjects—Educational Psychology, Tamil, English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Science—along with free NMMS practice tests and instant score lookups.

How do I unlock the TNTET All-Subject PRO Pass?

Sign in to tnclassroom.com using your Google (Gmail) account, click any locked PRO session, and complete the registration Google Form with your signed-in Gmail ID. Once activated, all subject sessions and MCQ quizzes unlock across both mobile and desktop devices.

Why does my TNTET PRO account show as Free on a desktop computer?

Check the email address displayed under your name in the top-right badge to confirm it matches the Gmail ID registered for your PRO pass. If you have multiple Gmail accounts signed into your desktop browser, sign out and choose your registered Pro Gmail account.

How can I check my past TNTET and NMMS quiz scores and download a Correction PDF?

At the end of every quiz, click Download Correction PDF to save a full answer-key sheet. To review past attempts anytime, visit tnclassroom.com/results.html and search using either your 10-digit mobile number or registered Gmail ID.
