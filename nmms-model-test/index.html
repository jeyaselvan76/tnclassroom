NMMS MAT & SAT Model Examination Center: Complete Practice Guide

The National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS) examination is a premier competitive test designed to identify and support meritorious Class 8 students across government and government-aided schools in Tamil Nadu. Securing a top qualifying score requires consistent practice across both papers: the Mental Ability Test (MAT) and the Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT).

At TN Classroom, our model test portal provides full-length mock examinations, topic-wise practice tests, and detailed solution worksheets structured according to the latest Tamil Nadu State Board (Samacheer Kalvi) syllabus and examination patterns.

NMMS Examination Structure & Mark Distribution

The scholarship examination consists of two distinct papers administered on the same day:

Test Paper Subject Coverage Total Questions Duration
Paper 1: MAT Verbal & Non-Verbal Logical Reasoning 90 MCQs (90 Marks) 90 Minutes
Paper 2: SAT Science (35), Maths (20), Social Science (35) 90 MCQs (90 Marks) 90 Minutes

Key Focus Areas for Practice Tests

MAT Reasoning Skills (மனத்திறன் தேர்வு)

  • Number and Alphabet Series (எண் மற்றும் எழுத்துத் தொடர்கள்)
  • Analogies and Classification (ஒப்புமை மற்றும் வகைப்படுத்துதல்)
  • Coding-Decoding and Venn Diagrams
  • Mirror & Water Images, Spatial Patterns

SAT Subject Mastery (படிப்பறிவுத் தேர்வு)

  • Class 7 & 8 Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Class 7 & 8 Mathematics Formulas & Arithmetic
  • Class 7 & 8 Social Science (History, Geography, Civics)
  • State Board Samacheer Kalvi Textbook Exercises
